Z-NATIVE / PC CONFIG SPEC — every setting marked GENERAL or SPECIFIC

GENERAL  = character-general: architecture/mechanism-level, do not retune
           per character.
SPECIFIC = character-specific: tuned against the current test character;
           documented default + sane range; re-check on a new LoRA
           (see CHARACTER-SWAP-CHECKLIST.txt).
Verdict column: measured facts are cited from results/run5/; judgement
lines are marked (J).
